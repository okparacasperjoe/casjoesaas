# ✦ Casjoe Business — Premium SaaS & AI Platform
## Version 1.0.0 (Initial Release)

Welcome to the future of enterprise automation. **Casjoe** is a premium, modular SaaS platform designed for high-performance business administration and market expansion, powered by advanced AI integrations.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

#### 1. AI Fashion Brain (Gemini Vision)
*   **Outfit Analysis**: Upload images for real-time AI styling advice.
*   **Trend Detection**: AI-driven insights based on user profile and preferences.

#### 2. Skincare Intelligence Hub
*   **Personalised Routines**: Morning and evening skincare guides tailored to skin type.
*   **Climate-Aware Matching**: Dynamic product recommendations based on tropical vs temperate climates.
*   **Ingredient Guide**: Interactive education on active ingredients (Retinol, Vitamin C, etc.).

#### 3. Professional Outfit Builder
*   **Drag-and-Drop Canvas**: Mix and match tops, bottoms, and accessories.
*   **Fit Score**: AI-calculated compatibility score based on body shape and style goals.
*   **Community Sharing**: Save and share looks directly to the social feed.

#### 4. Modular SaaS Architecture
*   **Multi-Tenancy**: Built to support multiple vendors/shops with isolated data.
*   **Clean URLs**: SEO-optimized, extension-free routing throughout the platform.
*   **i18n Ready**: Full translation support via central JSON language files.

---

### 🛠 Centralized Integration Hub
Administrators can configure all third-party services from a single dashboard:
*   **Payments**: Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Paystack, Flutterwave, and Monnify.
*   **Social Auth**: Google and LinkedIn Sign-In.
*   **Analytics**: Google Analytics 4 integration.
*   **Support**: Tawk.to live chat integration.

---

### 📦 Installation
1.  Upload all files to your server.
2.  Navigate to `yourdomain.com/install`.
3.  Enter your **Envato Purchase Code** to activate your domain.
4.  Follow the 4-step wizard to set up your database and admin account.

---

### 🔒 Security & Anti-Piracy
*   **Domain-Locked Activation**: Each purchase is securely tied to a single domain.
*   **Automated Updates**: Check for new features and security patches directly from the Admin Dashboard.
*   **CSRF & XSS Protection**: Industry-standard security layers for all forms and user inputs.
