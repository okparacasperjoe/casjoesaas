Welcome to Avellin

Avellin is a premium AI-driven digital fashion and beauty SaaS platform. Built on a modular PHP architecture, it allows entrepreneurs to launch a high-end fashion marketplace with integrated skin analysis and personalized styling.

Key Feature: Avellin uses Clean URLs by default. No .php or .html will ever appear in your browser's address bar.

1. Installation

Setting up Avellin is simple with our built-in Installation Wizard.

  1. Upload the script files to your server (preferably in the public_html or a subfolder).
  2. Ensure your domain points to the /public directory.
  3. Navigate to yourdomain.com/install.
  4. Follow the steps:
    • Requirement Check: System verifies PHP extensions.
    • Database: Enter your MySQL credentials.
    • Admin: Create your master administrator account.
Security: After installation, please delete the /public/install folder immediately.

2. Payment Gateways

Avellin supports global and regional payment providers out of the box. Go to Admin Dashboard > Settings > Integrations to configure:

Stripe

Enter your Publishable and Secret keys to enable global credit card payments.

PayPal

Enter your Client ID and Secret to accept PayPal payments.

Razorpay & Paystack

Optimized for Indian and African markets respectively. Simply toggle them ON and add your keys.

3. AI Fashion Brain

The core of Avellin is its AI integration. We support two major engines:

  • Google Gemini Vision: Used for skin analysis and body shape detection from photos.
  • OpenAI: Used for the interactive fashion chatbot and smart support responses.

Get your API keys from the Google AI Studio and OpenAI Dashboard.

4. Social Login

Enable "Login with Google" or "Login with Apple" to increase user conversion. All settings are manageable from the Integrations Hub in the Admin Panel.

5. Modular Architecture

Avellin is built to be extended. All specific fashion logic resides in:

app/Modules/Avellin/

You can easily add new modules or themes without touching the core SaaS engine.
